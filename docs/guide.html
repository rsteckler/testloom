User Guide

How to use the blog application

Viewing Blog Posts

When you first open the application, the home page displays a list of all published blog posts. Each entry shows the post title and the date it was created.

  1. Open the application in your browser at http://localhost:5173
  2. Browse the post list — posts are displayed in reverse chronological order, with the newest posts at the top.

Note: If no posts have been created yet, the home page will display an empty state prompting you to create your first post.

Reading a Blog Post

To read the full content of a blog post, simply click on its title in the post list.

  1. Find the post you want to read in the list on the home page.
  2. Click the post title to open the full article view.
  3. Read the article — the full content is displayed along with the title and publication date.
  4. Go back — use the back button or navigation link to return to the post list.

Creating a New Blog Post

Writing a new post is straightforward. You need a title and your article content.

  1. Click "New Post" — find this button on the home page or in the navigation area.
  2. Enter a title — type a clear, descriptive title for your blog post in the title field.
  3. Write your content — use the content area to write your full article. You can write as much or as little as you like.
  4. Save the post — click the "Save" or "Publish" button to submit your post. It will be sent to the backend API and stored.
  5. Verify — you will be redirected to the home page (or the post view) where your new post should now appear.

Important: Both the title and content fields are required. The save button will not work if either field is left empty.

Tips

  • Keep titles concise — a short, descriptive title helps readers find your post in the list.
  • Use paragraphs — break your content into paragraphs for easier reading.
  • Check the API directly — if you want to verify your posts are being stored, visit http://localhost:3000/api/posts in your browser to see the raw JSON data.
